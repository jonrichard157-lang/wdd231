Non-Profit (NP) Membership

Cost: Free (Proof of non-profit 501(c)(3) or CNPJ Social status required)

  • Official listing in Chamber Member Directory
  • Access to monthly community networking breakfasts
  • Free participation in quarterly non-profit workshops
  • Subscription to weekly regional business newsletters

Bronze Membership

Cost: $150 / year

  • Everything in Non-Profit tier
  • Enhanced directory listing with clickable website & social links
  • 10% discount on Chamber networking luncheons and seminars
  • Certificate of Chamber Membership & digital badge for your site
  • Job board posting privileges on Chamber career portal

Silver Membership

Cost: $300 / year

  • Everything in Bronze tier
  • Home Page Member Spotlight: Eligible for randomized rotation spotlights on the Chamber home page
  • 25% discount on Annual Tech & Sustainability Summit tickets
  • Quarterly feature in Chamber social media channels
  • Access to exclusive B2B networking dinners

Gold Membership

Cost: $500 / year

  • Everything in Silver tier
  • Top Tier Spotlight Priority: Premium placement on home page and event materials
  • Free VIP passes to all Chamber events throughout the year
  • Featured full-page spotlight interview in the Annual Business Review
  • Dedicated executive seat on the Chamber Advisory Board
  • Direct government advocacy and policy representation
